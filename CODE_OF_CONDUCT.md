# Code of conduct / 行動 規範

gukhan is a small project for readers, editors and learners of Korean. Everyone taking part,
in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith, including toward people who are new to Hanja, to Korean
  or to programming;
- keep discussion on the work. Disagreements about orthography or history are welcome when
  argued with sources; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination and slurs are not;
- not share other people's private information, and not post text they have no right to share.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's *Report content* on the item or
contact the maintainer (see the repository profile). Reports are handled in confidence.
